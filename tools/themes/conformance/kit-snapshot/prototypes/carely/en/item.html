How to take
Adults and children over 12 years: 200 mg to 400 mg with food, up to three times a day. Leave at least 6 hours between doses. Do not take more than 1200 mg in 24 hours.
Use for
Headache, migraine, dental pain, period pain, muscular pain and fever.

Warnings

  • Do not use if you have a stomach ulcer, or if you are allergic to ibuprofen, aspirin or other anti-inflammatory painkillers.
  • Take with food and use the lowest dose for the shortest time.
  • Ask a doctor first if you are pregnant or have asthma, or heart, kidney or liver problems.
  • Do not take with other anti-inflammatory painkillers.
  • Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
