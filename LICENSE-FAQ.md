# Licence FAQ

**I run my restaurant or shop on juwri. Do I pay anything?** No. The Community Edition is free under
the AGPL and includes one store of each type, with up to three branches per store, and unlimited
staff. For more stores or branches, use a juwri Chain licence or juwri Cloud.

**I changed juwri for my own business. Must I publish my changes?** Only if people outside your
organisation use your changed version over a network, which includes your customers using your
storefront. Then you must offer them its source code. Changes you keep internal need not be
published.

**Can I host juwri for other businesses and charge them?** The AGPL edition allows it if you share
your changes. Multi-tenant sign-up, selling plans, reseller and white-label tools are in
the Enterprise package, which needs an Enterprise subscription, priced per account (business) on
your platform.

**Can I test the Enterprise features?** Yes. Ask JUWRI LTD for evaluation access (sales@juwri.com);
development and testing are free under the Enterprise Licence.

**Do hotels, pharmacies, laundries need Enterprise?** No. Those modules, accounting integrations
and sales-channel connectors are in the free Community Edition. Connections that need JUWRI's
partner access to delivery apps or POS systems are a juwri Cloud service.

**What happens if my Enterprise subscription ends?** After the grace period in the Subscription
Terms, Enterprise features switch off. Your data stays, and the AGPL edition keeps running.

**Can I sell a theme or plugin?** Yes, under any licence you choose, if it uses only juwri's
documented extension points and APIs (see EXTENSION-EXCEPTION.md). The juwri Marketplace has its
own seller terms.

**Can I call my fork "juwri"?** No. Rename it and remove the logo; see TRADEMARKS.md. You may say it
is "based on juwri".

**I can't use the AGPL at all.** Ask JUWRI LTD about a commercial licence: sales@juwri.com.
