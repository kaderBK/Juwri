people can trust that something called juwri comes from JUWRI LTD.

- Say your product, service, theme or plugin "works with juwri", "is built on juwri" or "is for juwri".
- Show "Powered by juwri" with a link to juwri.com on a store running juwri.
- Run juwri, changed or unchanged, for your own business and call it juwri internally.
- Write about juwri and use the name in tutorials, reviews, talks and community events.
- Use the name in a social or community group, if it is clearly unofficial ("juwri users Riyadh").

- Register a domain, app-store listing, company name or social handle containing "juwri".
- Use the logo in a way that suggests JUWRI LTD endorses, certifies or partners with you.
- Sell merchandise with the name or logo.

## Forks and modified versions
If you distribute a modified version to others, give it a different name and remove the logo. You
may describe it as "based on juwri". Unmodified official releases may be redistributed under the name.

## Partners
Enterprise licensees, resellers, Marketplace sellers and Cloud partners receive trademark
permissions in their agreements (for example, "juwri Partner" badges). Resellers' white-label
sites must not use the juwri name unless their agreement allows it.
