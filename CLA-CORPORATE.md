# juwri Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement, v1.0

For companies whose employees or contractors contribute. Same terms as the Individual CLA
(§2–§5, §8, §9), granted by the Company for Contributions by its Designated Employees.

Additions to the Individual CLA:
1. The Company keeps Schedule A, a list of Designated Employees authorised to contribute, and
   updates it through the CLA bot's company admin page.
2. The Company confirms it owns, or may license, the Contributions of its Designated Employees.
3. The Company tells JUWRI when someone leaves the list. Contributions made earlier stay licensed.
4. Signed by an authorised signatory: name, title, date, company registration number.

Schedule A: Designated Employees (name, email, GitHub username).
