## 2. Providers that are not juwri Sub-processors

1. **Your own payment gateway.** If a store connects its own payment gateway account (for example a
card or wallet provider), the store contracts directly with that provider. It
processes payments for the store as the store's own processor or as an independent controller, and
juwri does not hold the store's funds. It is not a juwri Sub-processor.
2. **Your own delivery platforms and point-of-sale systems.** Where a store connects delivery apps
or POS systems under its own account and contract, those providers are the store's processors or
independent controllers. We send orders, menus, availability and status to them on the store's
instruction.
3. **Your own messaging, accounting or other integrations** that the store sets up with its own
credentials.
4. **Partner Relay partners.** Where the Partner Relay uses juwri's partner access to a delivery
platform or POS system, we treat the partner as the store's processor or independent controller,
not a juwri Sub-processor.

## 3. How we notify you of changes

1. We will give at least 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a Sub-processor (DPA section
5.2). We update this page and the "Last updated" date below, and we notify the account owner by
email.
2. **Subscribe:** to get an email whenever this list changes, send a request to privacy@juwri.com
   with the subject "Subscribe".
3. **Objecting:** if you object to a new Sub-processor on reasonable data protection grounds, write
to privacy@juwri.com within 30 days of our notice. We will work with you to resolve it, and if
we cannot, you may terminate the affected Services and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the
period after termination (DPA section 5.3).
4. **Urgent changes:** where we must replace a Sub-processor urgently for security or continuity, we
notify you as soon as we can, and your objection and termination rights still apply.
