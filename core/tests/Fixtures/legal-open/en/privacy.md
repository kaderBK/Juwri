# juwri Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **JUWRI LTD**, a company registered in England and Wales with
company number 14363043, whose registered office is at 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, United
Kingdom ("**juwri**", "**we**", "**us**"), uses personal data when we act as a **controller**, that
is, when we decide why and how the data is used.

It covers our website at juwri.com and its subdomains, the juwri Cloud sign-up and billing process,
our support, sales and marketing, our reseller, affiliate and Marketplace programmes, our
security-reporting channel, and our recruitment emails.

**Important: if you ordered food or goods from a store that runs on juwri.** That store, not juwri,
decides what happens to your data, and the store is the controller. juwri only processes the data
for the store, as its processor, under our Data Processing Addendum at https://juwri.com/legal/dpa.
Please read the store's own privacy notice, and contact the store to use your rights. See section
12.

## 3. Where we get personal data

- **From you**, when you sign up, buy, contact us, apply or report a security issue.
- **From your device**, through cookies and similar technologies, if you allow them where consent is
  needed.
- **From a store owner or reseller**, who may invite staff or provide contact details.
- **From service providers**, for example our payment provider (payment status), fraud and sanctions
  screening tools, and email or messaging providers (delivery and bounce data).
- **From public sources**, such as company registers and business websites, to prepare B2B outreach
  or check a reseller.
- **From referrers**, such as an affiliate or reseller who tells us about you.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (for example local storage and pixels) on juwri.com and in
the Cloud dashboard. We use only strictly necessary cookies without your consent. We use analytics
and marketing cookies only if you consent through the cookie banner.

| Type | What it does | Consent needed? |
|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | Keeps you logged in, protects forms (security tokens), remembers your cookie choices, balances load, remembers your language | No |
| Preferences and appearance | Remembers settings such as theme or layout, where they are not strictly necessary | Yes |
| Analytics | Counts visits and shows how pages are used so we can improve them. We use {{FILL:analytics_tool}} for this purpose only | Yes |
| Marketing and advertising | Measures campaigns and shows relevant ads, where we use them | Yes |

**Your choices.** When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies,
with equal prominence for both options. You can change your choice at any time through the "Cookie
settings" link at the foot of every page (https://juwri.com/cookies) or through your browser
settings. Rejecting non-essential cookies does not affect your use of our services. We keep a record
of your choice for 6 months before asking again.

A full list of the cookies we use, their purposes, providers and lifetimes is at
https://juwri.com/cookies.

**Stores on juwri.** A store's own storefront may use its own cookies, for example for the cart or
the store's analytics. The store is responsible for those and for its own cookie notice.

## 6. Who we share personal data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as follows:

- **Service providers (sub-processors and other suppliers)** that help us run juwri: hosting and
  storage, email and messaging, payment processing, customer support, analytics, security and
  monitoring, AI model providers for features you use, and accounting. They act on our instructions
  under contract. The list for juwri Cloud is at https://juwri.com/legal/subprocessors.
- **Stores and resellers.** If you are a store's staff user, the store owner (and, if the store is
  on a reseller's platform, the reseller) can see your name, role and login activity. If you contact
  us about a store, we may share your request with that store or its reseller to resolve it.
- **Payment providers and gateways** you or a store choose to connect, so payments can be processed.
- **Professional advisers**: lawyers, accountants, auditors and insurers, under duties of
  confidentiality.
- **Authorities and courts**, when the law requires it or to protect rights, safety and security,
  including fraud and abuse prevention. We will tell you first where we lawfully can.
- **Business transfers.** If we merge, are acquired or sell part of our business, the new owner may
  receive your data and must use it as set out here, or tell you if that changes.
- **Others with your consent**, or as you ask us to.

When we transfer personal data outside the UK, we use a lawful mechanism:

## 9. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit
and at rest, access controls, two-factor authentication for staff and owners, logging, backups, and
supplier checks. Details are at https://juwri.com/security. No system is perfectly secure. If a
breach is likely to put your rights at risk we will notify the regulator and affected people as the

## 10. Automated decisions and AI

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly
significant effects on you. We use automated tools for fraud and abuse prevention, but a person
reviews any decision that leads to suspending an Account.

Some juwri features use third-party AI models to generate content (for example item descriptions or
review replies) or to run agents for a store. Those features process content that a store supplies,
for that store, under our Data Processing Addendum. We do not use Customer Data to train AI models.
If we use AI on our own website (for example a support chatbot), we will tell you in the chat.

## 11. Your rights

**Under UK GDPR** you have the right to:

- be told how we use your data (this policy);
- **access** the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- have inaccurate data **corrected** and incomplete data completed;
- have your data **erased**, in some cases;
- **restrict** our use of your data, in some cases;
- **data portability**: receive data you gave us in a common format, where we use it by consent or
  contract;
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests, and at any time to direct marketing;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where we rely on it (this does not affect earlier use);
- not be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects (section 10);
- **complain** (see below).

**How to use your rights.** Email privacy@juwri.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We
answer within one month, which may be extended by up to two months for complex or numerous requests
(we will tell you). There is no fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. You can
unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time with the link in each email.

**How to complain.** You can complain to us first, at privacy@juwri.com. We will acknowledge your
complaint within 30 days of receiving it, look into it, and keep you informed. You also have the
right to complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at
https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/, without first complaining to us. If you live elsewhere, you can complain to your local data
protection authority.

**If you are in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait or Egypt.** Local laws may give you similar rights,
which may include the right to be informed, to access and receive a copy, to correct, to have data
deleted or its processing stopped or restricted, to withdraw consent, and, in Saudi Arabia, to
object to decisions based on automated processing. The same email works for any of them. You may
also contact your national authority: in Saudi Arabia the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA); in
the UAE the UAE Data Office; in Egypt the Personal Data Protection Centre; in Kuwait the
Communication and Information Technology Regulatory Authority (CITRA).

## 12. Stores' customers: juwri is the processor, the store is the controller

When you order from a restaurant, café, shop, hotel, pharmacy, laundry or other store that uses
juwri, the store decides what personal data it collects (for example name, phone, address, order,
loyalty and payment status), and why. The store is the controller. juwri provides the technology and
processes that data only on the store's instructions, under the Data Processing Addendum at
https://juwri.com/legal/dpa. We keep the data secure, use sub-processors listed at
https://juwri.com/legal/subprocessors, and delete or return it when the store's agreement ends.

What this means for you:
- **Find the store's privacy notice**, usually linked on its website or ordering page, and read it
  for your purposes, retention periods and rights.
- **Send rights requests to the store** (access, deletion, correction, marketing opt-out). If you
  send one to us, we will pass it to the store and tell you; we will not answer it ourselves unless
  the store asks us to.
- **Complaints** about how a store uses your data go to the store first, then the regulator that
  oversees it.
- If a store runs on a **reseller's** platform, the reseller may also be involved as the store's
  processor, and juwri acts for the reseller as a sub-processor.
- Stores in the Gulf and Egypt may have local registration or licensing duties as controller; those
  duties are theirs.

This section does not apply where juwri decides purposes and means itself (for example, if you
create your own juwri Account or contact us directly). Then section 1 to 11 applies.

## 15. Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait and Egypt

Our customers and prospects in these countries use juwri. Where local law applies to our own use of
personal data (as controller), we follow it. The key points are below. This section is a summary and
does not replace the rest of this policy.

- **Saudi Arabia (PDPL and Implementing Regulations).** The law may apply to the processing of
  residents' personal data by entities outside the Kingdom. Sections 2 to 11 explain why we collect
  data, what data, how it is collected, processed, stored and destroyed, and how to exercise your
  rights, and we make them available before we collect data from you.
- **UAE (Federal Decree-Law 45 of 2021).** The law may apply also to controllers outside the UAE
  that process data of individuals in the UAE. Sections 4, 6 and 7 explain the purposes, recipients
  and cross-border safeguards. Free zone regimes (DIFC, ADGM) may also apply to stores there.
- **Kuwait.** CITRA's data protection regulations may apply to providers of services in Kuwait.
  Where they apply to our own use of personal data, we follow them.
- **Egypt (Law 151 of 2020 and its Executive Regulations).** The law and regulations may require
  licences or permits, a local representative, and approval for transfers of data abroad. Where they
  apply to our own use of personal data, we will comply.
