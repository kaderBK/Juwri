# Link-in-bio page

Alongside your public menu and QR codes, every restaurant can turn on a
**link-in-bio page** — a single, mobile-first landing page that gathers your
menu, links, hours, socials, ordering, reservations and more in one place. It is
the link you put in your Instagram or TikTok bio, so a follower taps once and
finds everything.

Your bio page lives at your restaurant's web address followed by `/bio`, for
example `yourbrand.example/your-slug/bio`. It is separate from your menu, and it
stays hidden until you turn it on.

## Turning it on

Open **Bio Page** in the dashboard. Switch the page on, then either pick a starter
template — a ready-made set of blocks for a café, a restaurant, a delivery-led
spot, a fine-dining room, a minimal card, or a social grid — or start from an
empty page and add blocks yourself. A starter only seeds blocks; you can change
or remove any of them afterwards.

If the bio page is not available on your plan, the section is hidden. Contact
your platform owner if you expect it and don't see it.

## Choosing a design

The **Design** switcher offers a large gallery of ready-made looks. Tap any
design to preview it live on your own page above, then **Apply** — applying
changes only the look; your blocks stay exactly as they are. Some designs are
marked as available on a higher plan.

## Blocks

Blocks are the rows on your page. Add one from **Add a block**, where they are
grouped so the list stays easy to scan:

- **Content** — a plain link, a text note, an image, a divider, an announcement
  bar, a featured dish, a countdown, or a lead-capture (email) form.
- **Menu & food** — your full menu, a single menu item, a photo gallery
  (carousel or grid), or a set of FAQ questions and answers.
- **Visit & contact** — opening hours with a live "Open now / Closed" pill,
  a call / WhatsApp / SMS button set, a reservation button, a customer-reviews
  badge with a "Leave a review" link, a map, or an event with an RSVP.
- **Social & media** — your social icons, a video, a "buy a gift card" button,
  a loyalty / wallet-pass button, a grouped set of delivery-app buttons, or a
  tip jar.

Reorder blocks by dragging the handle. Hide a block without deleting it with its
visibility switch. Use the search box to filter a long list.

### Hours

Enter opening times per day (one or more ranges, comma-separated; leave a day
blank to mark it closed), or choose **Use my store hours** to pull the hours you
already keep for the restaurant. The page shows today's hours and whether you are
open right now, in your store timezone.

### Call, WhatsApp and SMS

Add any of a phone number, a WhatsApp number and an SMS number. Each shows its
own button, and each has a switch so you can hide a filled-in channel without
clearing it. Numbers with Arabic-Indic digits are handled correctly.

### Reservations

Point diners to your booking page by URL, or collect reservations over WhatsApp
with a pre-filled message. Add a short party-size note if you like.

### Delivery apps

List your ordering apps as "Name | link" lines — Uber Eats, DoorDash, Deliveroo,
Talabat, HungerStation and others get a built-in coloured badge; anything else
gets a generic one. Choose a list or a two-column grid.

### Gallery and FAQ

A **gallery** takes a "Caption | image URL" per line and shows a swipeable
carousel or a grid. An **FAQ** takes a "Question | Answer" per line and renders a
tap-to-open accordion.

### Gift card, loyalty and tip jar

**Gift card** and **loyalty** link to your existing store features when they are
active, or to a URL you provide. The **tip jar** links to your tipping page (with
optional preset amounts) and is a Pro block.

## Scheduling and highlighting a block

Every block can carry a **schedule** and a **highlight**:

- **Schedule** — set a start and end, and optionally the days of the week, to run
  a repeating window such as a happy hour, or an absolute date range for a
  seasonal block. Outside the window the block is hidden completely, not just
  dimmed. A bare end date keeps the block visible through the whole of that day.
- **Pin & highlight** — pin a block to the top of the page, and give it a gentle
  pulse or glow so it draws the eye. Motion is disabled automatically for anyone
  who prefers reduced motion.

## Appearance

The **Appearance** tab customises the look on top of your chosen design, and
saves through the normal settings save:

- **Colours** — accent, text and muted-text colours, and the button background.
- **Buttons** — fill / outline / soft style, sharp / rounded / pill corners, and
  left or centre alignment. You can also hide link icons and arrows.
- **Background** — a solid colour, a gradient, or one of several built-in
  patterns. An **image background** (with an adjustable dim overlay for
  legibility) is a Pro option.
- **Fonts** — pick a display font for headings and a body font from a curated
  list, including Arabic-capable faces. Pairing two different fonts is a Pro
  option.
- **Header** — avatar shape (circle, rounded square, square), an optional cover
  image, and switches to hide the title, tagline, status pill or verified badge.
- **Sharing & SEO** — a page title, description and share image for when the link
  is posted to social media, plus a favicon from your logo.

A contrast tip warns you if your text and background colours are too close to
read. Video backgrounds, uploaded custom fonts and a custom domain are shown as
"coming soon — contact support"; ask your platform owner to enable them.

## Insights

The **Insights** page shows how your bio page performs over a date range: total
views and clicks, the click-through rate, a QR-scan count, a leaderboard of your
top blocks by clicks, and a split of traffic by source and device. Every link
that leaves your page is tagged automatically so the visit shows up in your own
analytics.

**Country** and **top-referrer** breakdowns, and a **CSV export** of every click,
are part of the analytics add-on. Country data only appears once your platform
owner has enabled a geo source for the install; until then it reads as "not
available". No personal data — no IP addresses, no full referrer URLs — is ever
stored.

## What's free and what's Pro

The blocks above, the design gallery's basic looks, and the core appearance
controls (colours, buttons, solid/gradient/pattern backgrounds, curated fonts,
header options and sharing/SEO) are available to every bio-enabled plan. Image
backgrounds, extra font pairing and the tip jar are premium appearance
(**bio_pro**); country/referrer insights and CSV export are the analytics add-on
(**bio_analytics_pro**). Where a control is Pro, it shows a small badge; the limit
is always enforced, not just hidden.
