# Laundry and services

A **Laundry & services** store (see [Choosing your store type](store-types.md))
sells services rather than physical goods or dishes — dry cleaning, wash and
fold, alterations, or any other per-job business. Its catalog, booking flow,
and order board all use service-specific wording and pricing instead of a
restaurant's or a goods store's.

## Building your service catalog

Open **Services** in the sidebar to manage your catalog the same way you
would a menu or a product list — categories, then services inside them. Each
service has a **Name**, **Category**, **Description**, **Price**, an image,
and **Active**, plus two service-specific fields:

- **Pricing unit** — **Item** (a flat price per garment/job) or **Kilogram**
  (priced by weight — a wash-and-fold service, for example).
- **Turnaround** — how many hours the service typically takes, shown to
  customers as "ready in *N*h".

## How customers book

On your storefront, customers add services to a booking instead of a cart:
an each-priced service asks for a quantity, a per-kilogram service asks for
an estimated weight (in 0.1 kg steps). They then pick one of your enabled
[fulfillment methods](fulfillment-methods.md) — pickup, delivery, or an
appointment slot — and confirm. The customer's estimated weight sets the
initial price; see below for what happens once you weigh the item.

## The service board

Open your **Service board** (the laundry equivalent of a restaurant's
kitchen display) to track bookings through their lifecycle: **Received** →
**Processing** → **Ready** → **Out for delivery** → **Done**. Advance a
booking to its next state with one click, the same way a restaurant advances
an order on its kitchen board.

### Weight capture

For a per-kilogram booking, the service board lets you enter the **actual**
weighed amount once the item is in hand. Saving it re-prices the booking
line automatically from your per-kilogram rate — the customer's total
updates to match what you actually weighed, not their initial estimate, and
the change is recorded in your [activity log](activity-log.md).
