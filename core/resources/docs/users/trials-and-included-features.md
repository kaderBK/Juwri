# Trials, included features and cancelling

Some features are in your plan already, some can be bought on their own, and
some can be tried before you pay. This page explains what each of those looks
like in the dashboard, what happens when a trial ends, and how to stop paying
for something. For prices and plan comparisons, see
[Plans, billing and subscriptions](plans-billing.md).

You can see your current plan and trial in the dashboard under
**Apps & Add-ons → Extensions → Plan & subscription**.

## What the dashboard shows you

The sidebar tells you where you stand with every paid feature:

- **A normal row** — the feature is yours, open it and use it.
- **A dimmed row with a small lock and a price** — not in your plan yet. The
  row keeps the feature's own icon so you can still tell what it is, and the
  chip shows the cheapest way to get it (a price, or "Free trial" where one
  exists). Hovering shows what it does and what it costs.
- **A row with a small dot** — you gained this feature in the last two weeks.
  The dot disappears on its own after fourteen days.

Locked features are never hidden. If you cannot see a feature at all, it has
been switched off for the whole platform rather than withheld from you.

## Free trials

Where a feature offers a trial you get the whole thing for the trial period —
nothing is held back or limited.

While a trial is running:

- your dashboard shows a chip with the days remaining;
- in the last three days a reminder appears on the dashboard;
- we email you three days before it ends, and again the day before.

When it ends:

- **you are not charged automatically**;
- the feature switches off, and the dashboard says so;
- **your data stays exactly where it is**. Continue at any time and you pick up
  where you left off — nothing is deleted when a trial lapses.

To keep a feature past its trial, open the app or add-on from the reminder and
continue it there.

## "Included in your plan"

When you open a paid feature you already own, a green ribbon says so and names
your plan. There is nothing to buy — the feature is yours to switch on. Dismiss
the ribbon with the ✕ and it will not come back for that feature.

Some features need a few minutes of setup before they do anything useful.
Where that is true, the feature's own page lists the remaining steps and ticks
them off as you complete them. The list disappears once you are finished.

## Buying a feature on its own

A feature that is not in your plan can usually be bought without changing your
plan at all:

- **Add-on** — bought from the [Add-ons](plans-billing.md) page and billed with
  your subscription.
- **App** — installed from the app store; some apps include a free trial.
- **Plan upgrade** — where a feature only comes with a higher plan, its page
  says so and links to the plan comparison.

Where more than one route exists, the feature's page shows all of them side by
side with the real price of each, and leads with the free trial when there is
one.

## Reaching a limit

Plans include a number of stores, menu items and scans. When you reach one of
those limits the dashboard tells you which limit you hit and how many you have
used, and offers the shortest way past it — usually an add-on pack rather than
a whole plan upgrade.

## Stopping something you pay for

- **An app** — open it from **Apps** and uninstall it. Billing stops at the end
  of the current period; you keep it until then.
- **An add-on** — manage it from the **Extensions** page.
- **A plan** — change or cancel it from your **Subscription** page. See
  [Plans, billing and subscriptions](plans-billing.md) for what happens to your
  stores and menus after a downgrade.

## Turning off reminder emails

Every trial reminder has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Following it stops
those emails for your account. It does not change any trial: end dates stay the
same and the countdown still appears in your dashboard. The link works even if
you are not logged in on that device.

## If you would rather not see upsells at all

Your platform operator can switch the whole feature-spotlight system off for
everyone. If your dashboard shows no prices, tooltips or trial countdowns, that
switch is off — ask your operator. Entitlements are unaffected either way: you
keep everything you have paid for.
