# Merging duplicate customers

The same guest can end up with more than one customer record — they ordered
once as a guest, once with their phone, and once with a slightly different
spelling of their name. The **merge** tool folds duplicates into a single
customer so their history, spend and loyalty all live in one place.

## How merging works

Pick the **duplicate** record and the **canonical** record you want to keep.
Merging moves the duplicate's orders, wallet/store-credit balance, loyalty
progress and other history onto the canonical customer, then retires the
duplicate. The canonical record keeps its identity (name, contact details) and
inherits everything the duplicate carried, so no orders or credit are lost.

Merging is a housekeeping action, not a routine one — review the two records
first to be sure they are really the same person, because the duplicate is
consumed by the merge.

## When to use it

- Two profiles with the same phone number or email.
- A named account plus a guest account you can tell is the same person.
- Cleaning up before running a campaign or reading loyalty totals, so each guest
  is counted once.
