# Loyalty expiry and referrals

Two growth tools extend the stamps-and-points loyalty programme covered in
[Loyalty stamps](loyalty.md): letting loyalty points **expire** after a period
of inactivity, and rewarding customers who **refer** their friends.

## Loyalty point expiry

By default loyalty points never expire. If you would rather points lapse after
a period of inactivity — a common way to keep a programme active and cap your
liability — you can set an expiry window. Points that are not earned or redeemed
within the window expire, and customers are reminded before they lose them so
the programme still feels fair. Leave the window unset to keep the classic
never-expire behaviour.

## Referral rewards

The referral programme turns your existing customers into a growth channel.
Each customer gets a personal referral link or code to share. When a new
customer signs up or places a qualifying first order through that link, both
sides can be rewarded — for example store credit or loyalty points for the
referrer and a welcome discount for the friend. You control the reward and the
qualifying action, and the dashboard tracks who referred whom and which
referrals have paid out.

Referrals pair naturally with [store credit / wallet](wallet.md) (for cash-like
rewards) and [Loyalty stamps](loyalty.md) (for points-based rewards).
