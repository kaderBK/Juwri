# Choosing your store type

A store isn't only a restaurant menu anymore. When you create a store you
pick a **store type**, and that pick decides what the rest of the setup
wizard, the item form, and your public storefront look like. This page
explains the store types on offer, what changes with each one, and how to
tell what type a store already has.

## Where you pick it

Store type is the first thing you set, right at the top of **Step 1: Store
basics** on the new-store wizard (Workspace > Restaurants > New restaurant).
A row of cards lets you pick what you're selling:

| Store type | What you'll sell |
|---|---|
| **Restaurant** | Menu, dishes, dine-in / delivery. |
| **Retail store** | General products, ship or pickup. |
| **Cosmetics** | Beauty products with brands & shades. |
| **Clothing store** | Apparel with size & colour variants. |
| **Car parts** | Parts with vehicle fitment search. |
| **Laundry & services** | Per-item / per-kg services with pickup. |
| **Pharmacy (OTC)** | Over-the-counter products (no prescriptions). |
| **Café** | Coffee, drinks and bakes, order ahead (a food store, like Restaurant). |
| **Grocery store** | Groceries by weight or pack, delivery or pickup slot. |
| **Appointments and services** | Appointments in store or at home. |

Restaurant is the default and pre-selected pick, so an existing habit of
clicking straight through the wizard still creates a restaurant exactly as
before.

## What changes with your pick

The rest of the wizard adapts to your pick immediately:

- **Step 2 (template)** shows only the storefront themes built for your
  store type — a food menu template gallery for Restaurant, or the **Shelf**
  grid theme for every goods-type store (Retail, Cosmetics, Clothing, Car
  parts, Pharmacy), or the **Booking** theme for Laundry & services. See
  [Fulfillment methods](fulfillment-methods.md) for how each type's checkout
  works, and [Storefront themes and customizer](themes.md) for the theme
  picker in general.
- **The item form** changes shape: a restaurant gets the familiar dish form
  (photo, veg/non-veg, allergens, spice level); a goods store gets a product
  form (brand, price, weight, size/colour variants, stock); a laundry/service
  store gets a service form (per-item or per-kg pricing, turnaround time).
- **The wording throughout your dashboard** follows your pick — a goods
  store says "Products" and "Add to cart" where a restaurant says "Dishes"
  and "Add to order"; a service store says "Book" and "Bookings". You never
  see food-only fields (allergens, spice level, kitchen routing) on a
  non-restaurant store — they're not just hidden, they don't exist for that
  store type.
- **Available order types** narrow to what makes sense for what you sell —
  see [Fulfillment methods](fulfillment-methods.md).

Everything else about running the store — staff, orders list, analytics,
coupons, your team — works the same regardless of store type.

## Store type is set once, at creation

You cannot change a store's type after creating it — the item form, theme
set, and order types are all built around the pick. If you started the wrong
type, create a new store with the correct type rather than trying to convert
the existing one; nothing carries over automatically between them today.

## Plan availability

Every store type beyond Restaurant is part of a paid capability. If the
picker shows a type as unavailable, or creating a non-restaurant store points
you at an upgrade prompt, your current plan does not include it yet — see
[Plans, billing and subscriptions](plans-billing.md) or contact support to
add it. Restaurants are never limited by this — every plan can always create
a restaurant.
