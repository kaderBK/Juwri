# Pharmacy (OTC only)

A **Pharmacy** store (see [Choosing your store type](store-types.md)) is an
over-the-counter retail catalog — the exact same product form as
[Retail products](retail-products.md) (brands, price, weight, variants) —
plus two compliance tools: an age-gate flag and batch/expiry tracking.

**This platform does not support prescriptions.** There is no prescription
upload, no pharmacist verification, and no controlled-substance handling
anywhere in a pharmacy store. Everything you sell through it is treated as an
ordinary over-the-counter product.

## Compliance workspace

Open **Compliance** from the Products page to see every item with its
age-restriction and batch/expiry status in one table, plus:

- A warning banner when any item is past its expiry date, or expiring within
  30 days.
- **Compliance settings**: an **Automatically hide expired items from the
  storefront** toggle, and a **Storefront OTC disclaimer** text box — leave it
  blank to use the default wording ("This store sells over-the-counter (OTC)
  products only. This information is not medical advice — read the label and
  consult a healthcare professional before use."), or write your own.

Click **Edit** on an item to set, per item:

- **Age-restricted** — marks the item as requiring an age confirmation before
  a shopper can add it to their cart.
- **Batch** and **Expiry date** — free-text batch number and a date. An
  expired item is flagged **Expired**; one expiring within 30 days is flagged
  **Expiring soon**.

## What shoppers see

Your **Shelf**-themed storefront (see
[Storefront themes and customizer](themes.md)) shows your disclaimer text
near the top of the page. When a shopper opens an age-restricted item, they
see a confirmation prompt — "This item is age-restricted. Confirm you meet
the minimum age to buy it." — with **Confirm** and **Cancel** buttons, before
they can add it to their cart. If you turned on auto-hide, an expired item
simply stops showing on the storefront on its own.
