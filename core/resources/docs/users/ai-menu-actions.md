# AI menu actions

Beyond building a menu from a photo or PDF (see
[AI menu generator and menu scan](ai-menu-generator.md)), you can now ask AI to
**improve an existing menu** with targeted actions — rewriting descriptions,
suggesting prices, translating, tidying categories and more — without leaving
the menu editor.

## What the actions do

Typical AI menu actions include:

- **Rewrite descriptions** — turn a bare item name into an appetising,
  consistent description in your brand's tone.
- **Suggest or normalise prices** — get a sanity check on pricing across
  similar items.
- **Translate** — produce another language for an item or a whole category.
- **Tidy up** — fix capitalisation, spelling and formatting across the menu.

You stay in control: AI proposes the change and you review and apply it, so
nothing is edited without your say-so. Actions run on the items or categories
you choose, so you can improve one section at a time.

## Getting the best results

- Start from a real, saved menu — the AI works from your actual items.
- Review generated prices against your own margins before applying them.
- Keep dietary flags accurate by hand; AI descriptions are copy, not a
  substitute for the structured dietary data diners rely on.
