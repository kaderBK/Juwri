# Fulfillment methods

"Fulfillment method" is how a customer gets what they ordered. A restaurant
has always offered dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and (for hotels) room
service; a goods or services store offers a different set built for what it
sells. This page covers the non-restaurant methods and where you turn them
on.

## The methods

| Method | What it means | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| **Ship to address** | You ship the order to the customer's address. | Retail, Cosmetics, Clothing, Car parts, Pharmacy |
| **Pickup** | The customer picks a time slot and collects in person. | Retail, Cosmetics, Clothing, Car parts, Pharmacy, Laundry & services |
| **Appointment** | The customer books a time slot for the service itself. | Laundry & services, Appointments and services |
| **At home** | The provider comes to the customer: an address and a time slot are both needed. | Appointments and services |
| **In-store** | The customer buys while physically in your store; no address or slot needed. | Retail, Cosmetics, Clothing, Car parts, Pharmacy |
| **Delivery** | You deliver the finished job (or the groceries) to the customer. | Laundry & services, Grocery store (in addition to restaurants' own delivery) |

A restaurant's own methods (dine-in, takeaway, delivery, room service) are
unchanged and are managed from
[Checkout, tax and tipping settings](checkout-settings.md), not this page.

## Turning methods on

Open **Fulfillment** in the sidebar (only shown for a non-restaurant store)
to see every method your store type can offer, each as its own on/off
switch. A method that collects a shipping address is labelled "Collects a
shipping address at checkout"; one that needs a time slot is labelled
"Customer picks a time slot at checkout." Only the methods you switch on are
offered to shoppers on your storefront — turning everything off leaves
nothing to choose from, so keep at least one on.

## Order and service boards

Every non-restaurant order (except an in-store sale) moves through a board
in your sidebar — labelled **Fulfilment** for a goods store or **Service
board** for a laundry/services store — with states **New → Packing → Ready →
Shipped/Collected → Done** (a laundry booking shows the same states as
**Received → Processing → Ready → Out for delivery → Done**; see
[Laundry and services](laundry-services.md)). Advance an order to its next
state with one click, the same way a restaurant's kitchen board works.

## Tracking a shipped order

For a "Ship to address" order, open the order from your board above and
enter a **carrier** and **tracking number** to mark it shipped. This moves
the order forward on the board and lets the customer see the tracking info
on their order-tracking page.
