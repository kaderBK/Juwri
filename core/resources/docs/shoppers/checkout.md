# Checkout

Because every store in your bag is a separate business, checkout places a
**separate order with each store** — each with its own total and its own
payment. You still do it from one page.

## One cart or all of them

There are two ways in from the bag:

- **Checkout this cart** — every store's mini-cart has its own button, which
  checks out just that store.
- **Checkout all** — appears when two or more carts are ready, and places
  all the carts that can currently be checked out in one go. A cart that
  isn't ready (for example an unmet minimum order) simply stays in your bag
  for later.

## What checkout asks for

- **Name and phone** — always. You can check out as a guest; signing in
  first is what unlocks saved addresses, store credit and order history.
- **A delivery address** — required whenever any cart is set to delivery.
  Signed in, your default saved address is filled in for you; the delivery
  fee shown is calculated from its map pin, matching what the bag showed.
  Pickup and dine-in carts don't need an address.

## Shops and services with their own checkout

Checkout from your bag covers Delivery, Pickup and Dine in — the three
fulfillment choices a cart can carry. A store that ships products to your
address, has you book a pickup time slot, or books an appointment (for
example a laundry or another service business) completes checkout on that
store's own page rather than through your shared bag. You'll still choose
your items, your fulfillment method, and pay the same way described below —
it just happens on that one store's page instead of alongside your other
carts.

## Paying each store

Each store's card on the checkout page shows how you can pay **that store**:

- **Pay on delivery / at pickup** — always available, for every store.
- **Pay online** — shown only for stores that accept online payment. After
  you place the order, you're taken to that store's own payment page, which
  lists the payment methods the store supports.

If you hold **store credit** with a store, or the marketplace offers a
**wallet**, a toggle appears on that store's card so you can put it toward
the order first — see [Wallet and store credit](wallet-and-credit.md). When
a balance covers an order completely, there is nothing left to pay online.

Discount codes are applied in the **bag**, per store, before you reach
checkout — the discounted totals carry through.

## After you place the order

The confirmation page shows one card per order: the store, the total, its
payment state, and a tracking link. If an online payment didn't finish (you
closed the payment page, or it failed), the card keeps a **Pay now** button
so you can retry — the order isn't lost, and the page stays accurate even
after a refresh. A single **Track all** link opens live tracking for every
order you just placed.

If you checked out only one cart, the confirmation also reminds you about
any carts still waiting in your bag.
