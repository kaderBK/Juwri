# Wallet and store credit

You can hold two different kinds of balance here, and they are deliberately
kept separate: **store credit**, which belongs to one store, and — on
marketplaces that offer it — a **marketplace wallet** that works across
stores. They never merge into one number, because money that only spends at
one store isn't the same as money that spends anywhere.

## Store credit

Store credit is a balance a single store holds for you — typically from a
refund or a goodwill gesture. It only spends at that store.

- **Where you see it** — at checkout, on that store's card, once you're
  signed in and actually have a positive balance there. No balance means no
  row at all; you'll never see a teasing "0.00".
- **How it spends** — tick the toggle to put your credit toward that store's
  order. The exact amount applied is worked out when the order is placed:
  credit covers as much of the total as it can, and anything left over is
  paid the way you chose (on delivery, or online). If credit covers the
  whole order, there's nothing else to pay.
- **Why it needs sign-in** — credit is attached to your history with that
  store, which your phone-number account links up. A guest checkout can't
  see or spend it.

## The marketplace wallet

Some marketplaces also offer a wallet: one balance you can top up and then
spend at any store on the marketplace. If yours does, you'll find it in your
account area.

- **Per currency** — a wallet balance exists in a specific currency and only
  spends at stores that charge in that currency. If you hold money in more
  than one currency, each shows separately — they are never added together
  into one figure.
- **Topping up** — pick an amount and pay online. Your balance moves only
  after the payment actually completes.
- **Spending** — at checkout, a wallet toggle appears on each store card the
  wallet can pay. If the same balance could apply to several stores in one
  bag, checkout says so — the money can only be spent once, and each order
  draws on whatever is left.
- **Statement** — every movement (top-ups, payments) is listed in the wallet
  history, so the balance always has an explanation.

Store credit is applied before the wallet, and both reduce what's left to
pay — you're never charged for an amount a balance already covered.
