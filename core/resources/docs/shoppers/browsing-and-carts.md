# Browsing stores and your bag

This marketplace brings many stores into one place. You browse them all
together, but each store keeps its own menu, its own prices and its own
delivery arrangements — which is why your bag works the way it does: one bag,
with a separate cart inside it for every store you add from.

## Pick your city first

Everything starts with a city. The first time you open the marketplace you
are asked to choose one (or to share your location so it can be picked for
you). The stores, delivery fees and delivery areas you see all belong to that
city; you can change it at any time from the "Deliver to" control at the top
of the page.

## Finding stores and dishes

The home page gives you several ways in:

- **Search** — one search box covers both stores and dishes in your city.
- **Fulfillment tabs** — switch between Delivery, Pickup and Dine in to see
  the stores that offer each (a marketplace may not offer all three).
- **Categories and filters** — cuisine circles, plus quick filters for
  Favorites, Top Rated, Open now and Offers.
- **Sections and banners** — curated rails of stores and promotional
  banners, managed by the marketplace.

Some marketplaces also have an **Explore** tab: a directory of places by
city and cuisine, with a map. Listings there that are not yet taking orders
online still show their basic details.

## The store page

Opening a store shows its menu grouped by category, along with the practical
details before you commit: an estimated preparation/delivery time, the
delivery fee ("From …" until your exact address is known), the minimum order
amount if the store has one, and whether it is open right now. A closed
store still lets you browse — and you can **order for later** by scheduling
a time.

Store pages can also carry extras, depending on what the store has switched
on: discount codes you can copy and apply in your bag, a loyalty card,
reservations and waitlist, chat, and a Follow button. Tap an item to choose
its size or variant, options and extras, set a quantity, and add a note for
the kitchen (notes are text — type, don't record).

### Shops and service businesses

Not every store in the marketplace is a restaurant — some sell products
(clothing, cosmetics, car parts, and similar) or book services (like
laundry) instead of food. Their pages work the same way: browse the catalog,
tap an item, choose any size/colour or other options, and add it to your
bag. A shop's item page can show things a dish doesn't, like whether it's in
stock; a car-parts store may show which vehicles a part fits.

## How the bag works

Your bag can hold items from many stores at once. Inside the bag, each store
gets its own **mini-cart** with:

- its own items, subtotal and estimated time;
- its own **delivery fee** — based on the store's delivery zone and the
  distance to your address pin. Until you set an address the fee shows as a
  "from" estimate;
- its own **minimum order** progress bar, when the store has one;
- its own **fulfillment choice** — Delivery, Pickup or Dine in (with an
  optional table number), and a time: ASAP, or a scheduled slot picked from
  the store's opening hours over the next few days. If a store is closed,
  you'll be asked to schedule instead of ordering ASAP;
- its own **tip** for the courier (delivery orders only);
- its own **coupon** — a discount code always belongs to one store and is
  checked against that store's part of your bag. If a code stops qualifying
  (for example your cart drops below its minimum), the bag tells you why
  instead of silently dropping the discount.

A couple of things apply to the whole bag: a cutlery toggle, and in some
countries an optional round-up donation. You can clear one store's cart with
its own Clear control, or empty the whole bag at once.

Your bag's own fulfillment choice stays Delivery, Pickup or Dine in. A shop
or service business that ships to your address, has you pick a pickup time
slot, or books an appointment offers that directly on its own store page
instead of through your shared bag — see [Checkout](checkout.md).

## When checkout is blocked

The bag won't let you check out while any store's minimum order is unmet —
the progress bar shows how much is missing. And if your bag contains stores
that charge in different currencies, they can't be paid together: check out
each store's cart on its own instead.
