# Shopper guide

Help for ordering food through this marketplace — one place to browse many
stores, fill a cart from each, and check out. These pages cover the whole
journey, from picking your city to rating your order.

## Pages

- [Browsing stores and your bag](browsing-and-carts.md) — pick a city, find
  stores and dishes, and how one bag holds a separate cart for every store.
- [Checkout](checkout.md) — checking out one cart or all of them, delivery
  and pickup, fees, coupons, and how each store takes payment.
- [Wallet and store credit](wallet-and-credit.md) — the two kinds of balance
  you might hold, and how each one pays at checkout.
- [Tracking your orders](order-tracking.md) — the live tracking page, ETAs,
  driver contact, and your order history.
- [Your account and addresses](accounts-and-addresses.md) — signing in with
  your phone, saved addresses, favorites, and notifications.
- [Reviews, loyalty and following stores](reviews-and-loyalty.md) — rating
  stores and dishes, collecting loyalty rewards, and following stores you
  like.
