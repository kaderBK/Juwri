# Reviews, loyalty and following stores

Beyond ordering, there are three ways to have a relationship with a store
here: tell others what it's like, collect its loyalty rewards, and follow it
for updates.

## Reviewing a store

On a store's page, **Rate & review** opens the review form (on marketplaces
that have reviews switched on):

- an overall rating from 1 to 5 stars — the only required part;
- optional per-aspect scores for food, service, value and ambience;
- an optional written review (up to 2,000 characters) and up to three
  photos;
- your name, or a display name if you're not signed in.

Two honesty rules worth knowing. First, a **Verified order** badge appears
on your review only when it can be tied to an order that was actually
delivered to you — sign in with the phone number you ordered with and this
happens by itself; nothing you type can add the badge. Second, reviews may
be **held for moderation** before they appear, depending on how the
marketplace is set up, and you can only have one review awaiting moderation
per store at a time.

On other people's reviews, a **Helpful** button lets you upvote — once per
review.

## Rating what you ate

Separate from store reviews, the tracking page asks after each finished
order: a quick star rating for the order itself, and a 0–10 "would you
recommend" score with an optional comment. See
[Tracking your orders](order-tracking.md).

## Loyalty cards

Stores can run a loyalty programme — points or stamps. If a store you're
viewing has one, its loyalty card appears on the store page with the reward
on offer.

- **Anyone can see the programme.** Your personal balance appears once
  you're signed in *and* have ordered from that store — that first order is
  what starts your card.
- **Earning** happens with your orders at that store; the card shows your
  progress toward the reward threshold.
- **Redeeming** — when the card says the reward is unlocked, redeem it and
  the reward (typically a discount) is yours to use at that store.

Loyalty is always per store: points earned at one store never spend at
another.

## Following stores

On marketplaces that have following switched on, the **Follow** button on a
store page marks you as one of that store's followers — the follower count
is shown publicly on its page. Following requires signing in; tap the button
again to unfollow. Following is separate from hearting a store as a
favorite: favorites are a private shortcut for you, followers are a public
signal for the store.
