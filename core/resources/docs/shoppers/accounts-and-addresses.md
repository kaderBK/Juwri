# Your account and addresses

You can browse, fill a bag and even check out without an account. An account
is what makes the marketplace remember you: addresses, favorites, order
history, credit and loyalty balances.

## Signing in with your phone

There is no password. Enter your phone number, receive a one-time code by
SMS, type it in — you're signed in. The first sign-in creates your account
on the spot.

A few things follow from the phone number being your identity:

- **Your number can't be changed** from the profile. Everything — your order
  history, store credit, loyalty balances — hangs off it.
- **Your account belongs to the country you shopped in.** A local number is
  read against your browsing country, so the same digits in two countries
  are two separate accounts.
- **Anything already in your bag survives.** Sign in mid-shop and the bag
  you built as a guest merges into your account's bag, and any stores you
  hearted on this device are kept too.

Your profile holds your name, email and preferred language — all editable.
Sign out from the account page whenever you like, especially on a shared
device.

## Saved addresses

The address book lives in your account area. Each address is saved with a
map pin — the pin is what delivery fees and coverage are calculated from, so
place it accurately. You can:

- save as many addresses as you need and edit or delete them;
- mark a **default** — checkout fills it in automatically;
- keep addresses in different cities; the marketplace uses the right one for
  the city you're browsing.

If an address pin falls outside a store's delivery zones, that store can't
deliver there — the bag will show it rather than let you order into nowhere.

## Favorites and follows

Heart a store to pin it under the **Favorites** filter on the home page.
Hearts work without an account on one device; signing in makes them yours
everywhere. Following a store is separate — see
[Reviews, loyalty and following stores](reviews-and-loyalty.md).

## Notifications

The marketplace can send push notifications about your orders if you allow
it — you'll be asked in the app, and you can withdraw the permission the
same way. Signing in is required, since the notifications are about *your*
orders.
