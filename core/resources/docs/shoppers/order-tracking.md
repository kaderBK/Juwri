# Tracking your orders

Every order comes with a private tracking link — from the checkout
confirmation, from the Orders tab, and (if you ordered as a guest) from the
link you were shown when you placed the order. The link itself is the key:
nobody can look up your order without it, and you don't need an account to
use it.

## The live tracking page

Ordered from several stores at once? The **Track all** link on the
confirmation opens one page with a live card for every order. Each card
shows:

- **A status timeline** — from placed, through preparing and ready, to
  completed. It refreshes itself every few seconds; you don't need to
  reload.
- **An ETA** — the current best estimate for when your order will be ready
  or arrive.
- **Your courier, once one is assigned** — name and a tap-to-call phone
  number from the moment a driver takes the job. The driver's live position
  appears on the map **only while they're actually out delivering your
  order** — before pickup and after delivery it isn't shown, by design.
  Where a store uses an outside courier company, the card shows that
  courier's status (and a tracking link when the company provides one)
  instead.
- **A map** — the store, your delivery pin, and the driver while en route.

## Your order history

The **Orders** tab keeps your past orders across every store in one feed —
sign in with your phone number and any order you placed while signed in (or
that checkout linked to your number) is there, newest first, each with its
tracking link and a **Reorder** button that puts the still-available items
straight back into your bag.

## After delivery

Once an order is finished, the tracking page invites you to close the loop:

- **Rate your order** — a quick star rating for what you ate.
- **How likely are you to recommend** — a 0–10 score, with an optional
  comment. You can change your answer later; resubmitting simply updates it.
- **A Google review link** — shown only when the store has one.

More on ratings in [Reviews, loyalty and following stores](reviews-and-loyalty.md).
