## 1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 Words defined in the Terms have the same meaning here. In addition:

- **Applicable Data Protection Law**: all data protection and privacy law that applies to the
  processing of Customer Data under this DPA, including, as relevant, the UK GDPR, the Data

- **Customer Personal Data**: personal data contained in Customer Data that we process as your
  processor.
- **UK GDPR**: the retained version of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 as it forms part of the law of
  England and Wales under the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 and as amended, including by the
  Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.
- **EU GDPR**: Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
- **Controller, processor, data subject, personal data, personal data breach, processing,
  supervisory authority**: as defined in the UK GDPR, or the nearest equivalent term in the other
  Applicable Data Protection Law.
- **Restricted Transfer**: a transfer of Customer Personal Data that Applicable Data Protection Law
  restricts unless a safeguard or exception applies.
- **Sub-processor**: a third party we engage to process Customer Personal Data on your behalf.
- **Security Incident**: a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data in juwri Cloud.

1.2 If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on a data protection matter, this DPA prevails (Terms
§14.7). If Schedule 4 conflicts with the rest of this DPA for a customer in the country concerned,

## 6. Security incidents

6.1 **Notice.** We will notify you of a Security Incident without undue delay, and in any case
within 48 hours of confirming it (Terms §9.2, security page §8). "Confirming" means the point at
which we have reasonable certainty that Customer Personal Data in juwri Cloud was affected. We will
not delay notice because the investigation is still open.

6.2 **Content.** The notice will go to the account owner's email and security@juwri.com, and will
describe, as far as we then know:

- the nature of the incident, including the categories and approximate number of data subjects and
  records;
- the likely consequences;
- the measures we have taken or propose to take; and
- a contact point for more information.

We will provide further information in stages as we learn it.

6.3 **Response.** We will take reasonable steps to contain and investigate the incident, preserve
relevant evidence, and help you meet your own notification duties to regulators and data subjects.
You decide whether and how to notify regulators and individuals, except where the law requires us to
notify. We will not announce an incident naming you without your approval, unless the law requires.

6.4 **No admission.** Our notice or help is not an acknowledgement of fault or liability.

  them.

The Customer must not use free-text fields for special category data beyond what is stated above,
and we may restrict such use where it creates risk.

## Schedule 2: Technical and organisational measures

These are the measures we maintain for juwri Cloud, consistent with https://juwri.com/security
section 7.

1. **Hosting and location.** juwri Cloud runs on hosting.com in a single hosting region,
United States, for all customers.
2. **Encryption in transit.** All traffic uses HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or later.
3. **Encryption at rest.** Data is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider. Payment
gateway keys and other secrets that stores enter are additionally encrypted in the database with
application-level encryption.
4. **Payments.** Card details are entered on the payment provider's own pages or fields. juwri does
not store full card numbers.
5. **Tenant isolation.** Each business's data is separated by account and store at the application
level, with automated tests that check that one store cannot read another's data.
6. **Access control.** Staff access to production is limited to people who need it ("least
privilege"), protected by multi-factor authentication, logged, reviewed regularly and removed when
a person leaves.
7. **Customer account security.** Two-factor authentication and passkeys for owners and staff; staff
PIN login for point-of-sale devices; rate limiting and lock-out on sign-in.
8. **Backups and recovery.** We take encrypted backups at regular intervals, keep them for a limited
period, and test that we can restore from them from time to time.
9. **Logging and monitoring.** Security-relevant events are logged and monitored for abuse and
incidents. We aim to avoid storing full personal data in logs.
10. **Secure development.** Changes are reviewed and tested before release, with automated security
checks. Dependencies are monitored for known vulnerabilities and patched within the targets on the
security page (critical: 7 days, high: 30 days).
11. **Vulnerability management.** A public disclosure policy and a security contact at
security@juwri.com.
12. **Testing.** We test our software and infrastructure for vulnerabilities as described at
https://juwri.com/security.
13. **Personnel.** Confidentiality undertakings in contracts, security and data
protection training on joining and annually.
14. **Incident response.** A documented incident response process and the notice commitments in
section 6.
15. **Sub-processor management.** Security and privacy review of each Sub-processor before
engagement, and a written contract that flows down this DPA.
16. **Data minimisation and deletion.** Only the data needed for the Services is collected, with
deletion per section 10 and tools for Customers to delete records.
17. **Certifications.** We do not currently hold a security certification, and we do not claim one
until we hold it.
18. **Physical security.** Provided by the hosting provider.

**Part B. Transfers from the UK (UK GDPR Chapter V)**

B1. We will transfer Customer Personal Data outside the UK only if one of these applies:

- the destination is covered by UK adequacy regulations (including, where applicable, the UK

- another safeguard or exception that the UK GDPR recognises.

B2. Since 5 February 2026, the UK GDPR test for transfers subject to appropriate safeguards is that
the protection provided by the safeguards (and other means) "would not be materially lower" than the
UK standard (UK GDPR Article 46 as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025). We will carry out
and keep a transfer risk assessment for each Restricted Transfer to a Sub-processor, applying that

B3. The ICO's guidance states that the current IDTA and Addendum can still be used and that updated
versions are planned during 2026. We will move to updated versions when issued, and the parties

Three (processor to processor) or another valid mechanism applies.

Kingdom and its standard contractual clauses apply to transfers from the Kingdom. Where the Customer
is established in the Kingdom and sends Customer Personal Data to juwri or our Sub-processors

controller to a processor as an annex to this DPA, with Schedules 1 and 2 and the Sub-processor List

Sub-processor List. We do not currently offer hosting inside the Kingdom.

A5. **Assistance.** We will help the Customer meet its PDPL duties for data subject requests,

**Part B. United Arab Emirates**

B1. **Federal law.** Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL)
applies to the Customer to the extent its terms reach the Customer's processing. Where executive
regulations or guidance under it apply, we will work with you to apply them. In all cases you

B2. **Transfers.** The PDPL restricts transfers of personal data outside the UAE unless a condition

basis of this DPA and the safeguards in Schedule 3, as a contractual measure.

B3. **Free zones.** If the Customer is established in the Dubai International Financial Centre
(DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, as amended) or Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM Data
Protection Regulations 2021), that regime applies instead of the federal law, with its own transfer
tools, and the parties will use the free zone's standard clauses for transfers outside the zone, in

B4. **Breach.** Notifying the UAE Data Office and affected individuals of a breach, where the PDPL
requires it, is the Customer's responsibility as controller. Section 6 applies, and we will assist
you to meet any notification deadline that applies to you.
